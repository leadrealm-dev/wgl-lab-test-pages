Services

We keep the list short so we can do each job well. Most of our customers have us for the fall cleanup and then keep us on for snow.

Fall

Leaf cleanups, one visit or every two weeks until the trees are bare. Core aeration and overseeding in early fall. Bed edging, mulch and shrub trimming. Sprinkler winterizing with compressed air before the first freeze.

Winter

Driveway plowing at two inches, walks and steps shoveled and salted, and a second pass after the city plow fills the end of your drive. Seasonal contracts or per-visit service for the same price either way.
