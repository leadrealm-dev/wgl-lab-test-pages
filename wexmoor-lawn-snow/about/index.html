About us

We started with one truck, one mower and a handful of houses on our own block. People kept asking if we could also do their leaves, and then their driveways, and that is how a summer job turned into a year-round business.

We are still a small crew. The same people show up at your house every week, they know where the dog stays and which corner of the yard floods. We would rather do forty houses well than two hundred in a rush.

When it snows overnight we are out by four in the morning. We plow, shovel and salt in the order of the route, and we text you if something changes.
